      Why this matters

      A password policy sets minimum rules (length, complexity, no common passwords) so accounts are harder to guess. Passwords are one form of access control: they protect confidentiality in the CIA triad by making sure only authorised people can see your data. Weak or reused passwords are a leading cause of account takeover.
